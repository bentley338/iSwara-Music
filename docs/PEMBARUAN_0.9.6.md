# iSwara Music 0.9.6

- Identify songs without entering a token or signing in. Open **Search → Identify song**, then choose the microphone, device audio, or an audio/video file.
- Device audio requires Android 10 or later and Android's consent prompt. After granting consent, switch to the app playing the sound. iSwara records audio only and stops after a short sample.
- Share a TikTok link to iSwara, choose **Identify from TikTok**, then let the video play after granting consent. If the source does not permit audio capture, use the microphone with speaker playback or select a saved video that you can access.
- Recognition uses the server's AudD credential and a shared request limit. Audio samples are not retained by iSwara's recognition server.
