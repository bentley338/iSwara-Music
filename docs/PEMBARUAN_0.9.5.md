# iSwara Music 0.9.5

- Use **Identify song** on the Search tab to identify a song playing nearby or in an audio file. This sends a short sample directly to AudD and requires your own AudD token. The app does not save the token.
- Use **Search lyrics** to find a line in lyrics already cached or imported on this device. Tap a timed result to start playback at that line.
- Lyrics lookup now checks the track, artist and recording version across LRCLIB, KuGou and NetEase. When the lyrics match but their timing appears incompatible with the recording, the app displays plain text.
- Show less from an artist, hide a recording from suggestions for 30 days, or check why a track was suggested. These preferences stay on this device and are separate for each account.
- Shuffle favors tracks you have not played recently and spreads out tracks by the same artist. Refine a Mix for a more relaxed selection or more unfamiliar artists, and pin the tracks you want to keep.
- Play a 15-second preview from a track menu without replacing your playback queue.
