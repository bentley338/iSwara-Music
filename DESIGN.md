# iSwara design direction

Audience: everyday music listeners, initially Indonesian, on Android phones and tablets.
Identity: an original S formed from a continuous sound ribbon. The rhythm of the S appears in the icon and the player progress accent.
Palette: charcoal #151619, warm paper #FAF7F2, orange #FF8954. Orange marks playback and primary actions; album artwork provides content color.
Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans, chosen for open counters, readable compact metadata, and friendly rounded titles.
Layout: artwork leads discovery; compact rows lead collection management. A persistent mini-player keeps playback within reach.
Themes: paired warm light and charcoal dark; system is the default.
Motion: 200ms player expansion and state changes, honoring disabled system animations.
Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
Icons: Material rounded semantic controls, familiar on Android; the logo is custom vector artwork.
Spacing: 4/8dp scale, 20dp phone gutters, 48dp minimum controls. Rounded artwork and rectangular list structure distinguish content from actions.
